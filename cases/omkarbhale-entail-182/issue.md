# The board: one place for every repo and every call

Part of the rebuild in #178 (phase 1). The board is the owner's one place for every repo and every call entail makes: what is waiting on them, what entail decided and why, and the few settings that matter.

### Design first

The board is designed with the owner on a mockup before it is built: the pages, what an item reads like, how a call is confirmed or overridden, how a question is answered, how the log reads, and how the owner learns that something is waiting. The mockup uses the six real tickets entail has judged in this repo as content. The build issue follows the owner's review of the mockup.

### What it must hold

- **Waiting on you**, across all repos: calls to confirm while a repo is in confirm mode, and questions to answer. Each carries what the ticket was, what entail concluded, why it was raised, and the vision line or past case it rests on.
- **Answering** in one path: confirm, override with a reason, or answer the question, recorded under the owner's name and fed back to the issue and the case book.
- **The log** of every call with its reason, per repo and across repos.
- **Repos**: enroll, unenroll, confirm mode on or off, the model and effort used.

### Not on the board

Whether to start, pause or reorder work. The board holds product decisions only.
