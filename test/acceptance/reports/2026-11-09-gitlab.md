## Why T11 and T13 did not merge

Both PRs passed CI and every mechanical check. The landing grade then marked a criterion not met:

- **T11** (`!1`, graded twice). Its criterion C2 restates the vision line it cites, "A word is a run of characters between whitespace": *any* whitespace separates words. The harness's prepared change for T11 alone splits only on ASCII whitespace. Its evidence says that "which other Unicode characters separate words is not changed here". The grade: C2 not met.
- **T13** (`!3`, graded twice). Its criterion C2 is the same vision line. The harness's prepared change for T13 alone splits on each single Unicode whitespace character (`split(/\p{White_Space}/u).length`). Runs of whitespace and leading or trailing whitespace therefore still add empty words. Its evidence says that "runs of spaces count as they did before". The grade: C2 not met. The grade also quoted evidence for C1 and C3 with ` ` written as the character itself rather than the escape the PR holds. entail's own word-for-word check caught that and refused the quote.

On these facts the grader is right. `test/acceptance/prepared.ts` deliberately splits the word definition between the two tickets. T11 alone handles ASCII runs, T13 alone handles single Unicode characters, and they are meant to meet in `\p{White_Space}+` once one of them merges. But entail's criteria for each ticket ask for the whole vision line, so neither can merge first. The executor re-posts the same evidence, and both PRs wait until the run stalls. This is in the harness's prepared changes, not in entail and not in anything specific to gitlab.com. GitHub run 1 of the GitHub half, running at the same time, ended with T11 and T13 at landing too. No product bug was filed for it.

Every GitLab-specific part worked: setup and reset of the gitlab.com fixture, merge requests, pipelines and their checks at the head, labels, comments, T1's first PR failing CI and the executor pushing the fix once entail named the failing head, the rebuilds after other tickets merged, merging at the verified head (check 7), and the restart with nothing lost or duplicated (check 9).
