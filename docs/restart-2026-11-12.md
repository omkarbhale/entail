# The restart of 2026-11-12

On 2026-11-12 entail was started over in this repository. The tree before the restart is in git history at commit `5c57ba2`, the way the restart of 2026-11-08 left its own predecessor at `f336238`. Nothing was rewritten or force-pushed; anything from the old tree can be copied back from that commit.

## Why

The first build made wrong calls, and an investigation of those calls found three causes.

1. **It asked the wrong question.** entail asked whether the vision *forbids* a ticket, never whether the ticket makes the product *better*. Its verdict could not say "no": a ticket the vision did not contradict was cleared, even where the vision was silent. The vision said so in as many words, and the build implemented it faithfully.
2. **Its judgment was fenced with mechanical gates that reacted to wording, not substance.** Pins matched on keywords. Criteria had to come from a closed list of file-glob checks. A second model was told to overturn the first. The landing grade failed a PR for any visible change its criteria had not named, even when that change was the necessary consequence of a criterion being met.
3. **It was specified and built in a day from a spec, and only ever tested against that spec.** No real ticket and no call the owner actually made ever checked whether the product judged well.

## What the rebuild is built around

Two judgments, each made as the owner would make it, each recorded with its reason.

- **One per issue: go, no, or ask.** Go comes with a plain reason and the few criteria an implementer would not guess. No closes the ticket with the reason, whoever filed it; a ticket that plainly goes against a vision line is closed quoting that line, and reopening it through `/entail` brings it to the owner. Ask goes to the owner when entail cannot tell whether the change is better, or when the change is large or hard to undo. Where the vision speaks it decides; where it is silent entail judges on the merits.
- **One per PR: merge, send back, or ask.** A PR merges when it solves its ticket's problem, meets what was asked and leaves the product better. Facts a machine can read, such as CI results and changed files, come from the forge, never from the PR's own claims; whether the change is good is judged by a model that did not write it.

How entail judges is measured against a case book of real tickets and the owner's own calls on them, and a change to the judging is checked by replaying that book. The amended VISION.md states all of this; it is the owner's word, and this page only explains the reset.

## What carried over

Only modules the two-judgment loop needs and that are free of the old judging design, with their unit and recorded-contract tests:

- **The GitHub forge adapter and its CLI transport** (`src/forge/`): the forge interface, the GitHub adapter, the `gh api` transport, and the factory, now without the GitLab path. With them the change deduplicator and the cursor store (`src/changes.ts`, `src/cursors.ts`), which the adapter's contract test exercises.
- **The model runner** (`src/model-runner.ts`, `src/claude-runner.ts`, `src/schema.ts`, and `src/scripted-runner.ts` for tests). The fixed list of run kinds from the old design is gone; a run's kind is whatever the judgment asking for it names.
- **Read-only checkouts and vision reading** (`src/mirror.ts`, `src/git.ts`, `src/vision.ts`).
- **Comment rules** (`src/comment-rules.ts`), **the lock** (`src/lock.ts`), **the operational log** and **the data directory** (`src/operational-log.ts`, `src/data-directory.ts`).

Comments in these files that cited the deleted decision records and spec were rewritten to stand on their own.

## What stayed in history

The intake and landing judgments and everything fenced around them (verdict checks, challenge, criteria, landing grade, pins, citations), the store and its records repo, the pass and its scheduling, the board and every other page, the server and CLI, health and halts, notices, the service installer, enrollment, repo settings and switches, the GitLab adapter and its recordings, the acceptance harness and its fixture repo, the 21 decision records, the spec, the glossary, and the board screenshots. None of it is needed to start the rebuild, and any of it can be copied back from `5c57ba2` when it is missed.

This page replaces the decision records. The rebuild writes no spec first; it writes the two judgments and the case book that measures them.
