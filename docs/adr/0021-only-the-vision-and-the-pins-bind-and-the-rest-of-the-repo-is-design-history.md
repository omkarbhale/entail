# Only the vision and the pins bind, and the rest of the repo is design history

Only VISION.md and the repo's pins decide whether a ticket is cleared or goes to the owner. Everything else in the repo is **design history**: decision records such as `docs/adr/`, a spec such as `docs/spec/mvp-1.md`, other docs, tests and the code itself. Design history may inform how a change is built. It is never a line a ticket contradicts, it never makes readings consequential, and criteria never lock a ticket in or narrow it to what design history describes. When a ticket overturns a past decision, its criteria require updating or superseding the record of that decision. Beyond the vision and the pins, the decider goes by a general understanding of what improves the project.

This came from [#169](https://github.com/omkarbhale/entail/issues/169). entail's first criteria for it locked in "the board stays server-rendered HTML with no front-end framework and no UI build step, as ADR 0017 and the MVP-1 spec describe". When the issue was edited to ask for a front-end framework, the re-check raised the change to the board as a consequential call instead of clearing it. The owner's note on that board answer: "Entail made reasoning here based on ADRs and MVP-1 criteria, but failed to accept issues that improve a product even though the request was an improvement to the product in my opinion". The owner then decided [#170](https://github.com/omkarbhale/entail/issues/170) on the board:

> Only VISION.md and the pins decide whether a ticket clears or goes to the owner. ADRs and the MVP-1 spec may still inform how the change is built, but they never make readings consequential or lock criteria. When a ticket overturns a past decision, its criteria require updating or superseding that ADR.
> A ticket like the edited #169, which asks for a front-end framework against ADR 0017, is cleared, and its criteria include superseding ADR 0017. It is not raised to the board.
>
> This isn't specific to ADRs which were in this repo. It can be anything, tests, documents, code, etc. Everything is up for improvement and nothing is binding other than vision.md and _the model's general understanding of what is an improvement._

VISION.md already says a ticket comes back to the owner only when it contradicts the vision, touches a pin, or has readings the vision makes consequential, and that what matters for a project is said in its vision and pins, not built into entail. The decider, the challenge and the criteria writer read a checkout of the repo, though, and nothing told them that a decision record or the spec does not speak for the owner the way the vision does. This record says so.

## Considered options

- **Treat decision records like vision lines**: rejected by the owner. A project's design history would then block every improvement that changes a past design, and the vision would stop being the only statement of what the owner wants.
- **Hide design history from the model runs**: rejected. It still tells a run how a change should be built, what already exists, and which record a change makes stale. The runs keep reading the whole checkout.
- **Let the owner pin decision records**: not needed for this. A pin can already name any path or topic, so an owner who wants a record to hold can pin it, and the pin then binds like any other.
- **Set aside a challenge objection whose quote is not word for word a line of VISION.md**: rejected. It would catch an objection that rests on a decision record mechanically. But a challenger that slightly misquotes a real vision line, as a model can, would then have its objection set aside and the ticket cleared. That trades over-asking for wrongly clearing, which VISION.md forbids. The rule reaches the challenger through its prompt only, and every objection counts as before.

## The case it fixes and what it could break

VISION.md asks a change to how entail judges to name the case it fixes and the cases it could break, and to move only as far as that case needs.

- **The case it fixes** is #169's. Criteria were locked to ADR 0017 and the MVP-1 spec, and an edit asking for a front-end framework was raised to the board as a consequential call when the vision is silent on it and no pin covers it. The decider, re-check, challenge and criteria writer are now told that design history never binds. A criteria output that lists a record the ticket overturns without a criterion to supersede it is invalid. Unit tests run each prompt for every `tally` fixture ticket and an edited-#169 case through the re-check.
- **What it could break, and why it is held to that.**
  - *A wrong clear.* A ticket that only goes against a design decision is now cleared. That is the owner's decision here. A project that wants a design decision to hold says so in its vision or a pin. Nothing that sends a vision or pin call to the owner changed mechanically: the checks on a verdict, pin matching, the challenge's objection kinds and what makes an objection concrete are as they were, and the prompts still tell every run that a contradicted vision line, a touched pin or readings the vision makes consequential need the owner. So a ticket that contradicts the vision, touches a pin or has readings the vision makes consequential still comes to the owner.
  - *Over-asking.* Nothing new raises a ticket. The new criteria check makes output invalid, which is a machine failure and is retried. It never goes to the board.
  - *A ticket that cannot clear.* A model that keeps naming an overturned record without a criterion to supersede it, or that needs a sixth criterion for it, keeps failing the check and the ticket stays deciding while it is retried. That is visible on the Calls page and in the operational log, and it clears nothing wrongly.
- **Acceptance.** The `tally` fixture has no decision records, so none of T1–T13 should list one and their expected paths do not change. The live acceptance run is made by hand, as its README says (ADR-0010).

## Consequences

- **One rule, in every judging prompt.** The verdict, re-check, challenge and criteria prompts all carry the same paragraph: only the vision of record and the pins decide whether a ticket goes ahead or comes to the owner, and decision records, a spec, other docs, tests and code are design history. The verdict prompt adds that only a vision line can be contradicted, and that design history never bears on a difference between readings. The re-check prompt adds that an edit asking to overturn design history goes ahead differently, with new criteria, when no vision line opposes it and no pin is touched. Each prompt's version is bumped.
- **The challenge.** Its prompt says that only a line of VISION.md can be contradicted, that design history never bears on a missed reading, and that a `criterion_beyond_source` objection covers a criterion that locks the ticket to what design history describes. A criterion that requires superseding a record the ticket overturns is not beyond its source. What makes an objection concrete is unchanged (issue #156).
- **Criteria.** A criterion's sources stay the issue, its findings, a vision line or the owner's answer. Design history is never a source. The criteria output gains `records_overturned`, each record of a past decision the ticket overturns, with its path and the decision it records. A new check before clearing requires that each one is matched by a criterion's `changes_path` glob, either the record's own path or its directory such as `docs/adr/**`. Output that fails the check is invalid, which is a machine failure and is retried. It never goes to the board. The records are kept on the criteria call.
- **What still comes to the owner is unchanged.** A ticket that contradicts the vision, touches a pin, or has readings the vision makes consequential still needs the owner, unless the owner's answers settle it (ADR-0008). The mechanical checks on a verdict are unchanged.
- **Amends** ADR-0005 (criteria), ADR-0011 (the order of judgment and the challenge) and sections 6 and 7 of the MVP-1 spec.
- Decided by the owner on [entail treats decision records and the spec like the vision, and resists improvements](https://github.com/omkarbhale/entail/issues/170).
